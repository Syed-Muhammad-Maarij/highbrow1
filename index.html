Our Philosophy

Ambition nurtured. Achievement celebrated.

Highbrow College values tolerance, diversity and compassion. We welcome students from varied social, academic and economic backgrounds and shape them into confident, principled, forward-thinking young adults.

Beyond the classroom we put real weight on extracurricular life, social service, discipline, respect and the pursuit of excellence. That's why our students carry the name Phoenixes.

Admissions

Your journey to Highbrow

Seats are limited and admission is competitive. Apply early in Round 1 to unlock Early Bird benefits.

I

Enquire & Visit

Send an online enquiry or book a campus visit with the Admissions Office.

II

Apply Online

Complete the official admission form for your session (A Level 2027–29 / O Level 2027–28).

III

Assessment

The college reviews your grades and extracurricular record. Scholarships are decided on merit.

IV

Join a House

Confirm your seat, meet your teachers and become a Phoenix of your house.

Key Information
    Early Bird · Round 1
    • ✦Teacher preference: choose 2 of your teachers; the college assigns 1
    • ✦30% off the admission fee
    • ✦Confirmed +10% scholarship on top of grade- and ECA-based awards
